Spanakopita

Description

Spanakopita is a traditional Greek savory pie made with crispy, flaky phyllo pastry layers and a rich filling of chopped spinach, salty feta cheese, onions or scallions, fresh herbs like dill and parsley, and bound with eggs

Ingredients

  • 1kg baby spinach
  • 50g fresh mint, chopped
  • 10 spring onions, chopped
  • 100g dill or fennel, chopped
  • 600g smooth feta
  • 2 rounds hard feta
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 packet puff pastry
  • 60g unsalted butter

Steps

  1. Boil the baby spinach in a pot until well-cooked. Remove and strain.
  2. Melt the unsalted butter in a pot and fry the spring onion.
  3. Add the mint and dill to the pot and sauté. Remove from heat, add the spinach and mix well.
  4. Place all contents in a mixing dish.
  5. Add smooth feta cheese and crumble the hard feta cheese.
  6. Add the eggs and mix into the spinach mixture.
  7. Butter the puff pastry and place on a sheet covering the bottom of the oven-proof dish. Perforate the pastry with a fork.
  8. Pour the mixture onto the puff pastry, levveling it evenly.
  9. Cover with another layer of buttered puff pastry and cook at 180°C for approximately 2 hours.
