Sherried Chicken

Description

Sherried chicken is tender chicken cooked in a rich sauce made with dry sherry, cream, mushrooms, garlic, and herbs. It has a creamy, savory flavor with a subtle sweetness from the sherry.

Ingredients

  • 8 chicken leg pieces
  • 75g butter
  • 125g mushrooms
  • 25ml flour
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 25ml sherry
  • 50ml yoghurt
  • Green olives
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Season chicken well with salt and pepper and fry in butter for approximately 25-30 minutes until cooked through.
  2. Remove from the pan and immediately arrange in a serving dish and keep warm.
  3. Add flour and gradually stir in the chicken stock and sherry.
  4. Cook until thickened then add yoghurt.
  5. Pour over chicken.
  6. Garnish with green olives.
  7. Serve immediately with sides of your choice.
